02 — how it's made

  1. 01 — the impression

    you bite.

    We send you a kit, or you come to Milano. Warm putty, thirty seconds, a perfect copy of every tooth.

  2. 02 — the wax

    we carve.

    Each grill is sculpted in wax on your copy, by hand, until the edges are as sharp as you asked for.

  3. 03 — the cast

    we pour.

    The wax burns out, the silver goes in. Then filing, setting, polishing: two weeks of making it look effortless.

  4. 04 — the fit

    you smile.

    It clicks on and stays on. You can take it off whenever you want. You won't want to.
